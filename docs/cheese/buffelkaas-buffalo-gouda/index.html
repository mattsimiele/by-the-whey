Buffelkaas (Buffalo Gouda)

Made by Treur Kaas · Woerden, Netherlands

★ —Not rated yet

This is a young Gouda style cheese made with Dutch buffalo milk, aged for about 8 weeks. Because it’s younger, it keeps that soft, elastic texture and super approachable flavor. The high fat content from the buffalo milk gives it that lush, almost melt-in-your-mouth quality without needing long aging. It’s a great intro cheese that still feels special.
