Tête de Moine

Made by AOP-regulated (multiple producers; specific dairy ID varies by wheel) · Bellelay, Jura Mountains, Switzerland

★ —Not rated yet

This is “monk’s head” cheese, originally made by monks at the Abbey of Bellelay over 800 years ago. Instead of cutting it into wedges, you shave it on a girolle into little ruffled rosettes. That shaving isn’t just for looks, it actually releases more aroma and makes the texture feel softer and almost melt-in-your-mouth. The rind is washed in brine, giving it that sticky orange exterior and gentle funk. Historically, wheels were even used as currency.
