Drunken Goat

Made by Mitica (the import arm of Forever Cheese) · Not provided by creamery, Jumilla, Spain

★ —Not rated yet

This is a name branded version of D.O.P Queso de Murcia al Vino. It is a semi-firm goat cheese soaked in the traditional style wine of the region, Doble Pasta, which is made from Monastrell grapes. This is a very starter-friendly cheese for new customers / a good option for people who “don’t like goat cheese”
