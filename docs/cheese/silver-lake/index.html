Alpine

Silver Lake

Made by East Hill Creamery · Perry, NY, USA

★ —Not rated yet

A New York take on classic Alpine cheeses like Comté and Gruyère. Made in large-format wheels and aged on wood boards for at least a year, this cheese leans into that sweet-savory balance with a big umami punch. It’s a local favorite for a reason and consistently wins awards at the New York State Fair.
