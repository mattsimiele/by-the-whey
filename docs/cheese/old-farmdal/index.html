Gouda

Old Farmdal

Made by Deweer Family at Kaasboerderij’T Groendal · West Flanders, Belgium

★ —Not rated yet

Old Farmdal was created by the Deweer family as a younger counterpart to their well-known OG Kristal Gouda. Using the same milk and core process, they adjusted the cultures to develop a similar depth of flavor in less time. It’s aged about half as long but still delivers those crunchy crystals and layered sweetness, making it a great “in-between” Gouda.

Flavor profile

  • Dense and slightly fudgy with pronounced tyrosine crystals throughout. Sweet and savory with notes of caramel
  • stone fruit
  • and a subtle “boozy” cherry quality. Finishes with a light acidity reminiscent of dark chocolate.

Pair it with

  • prosciutto
  • amber ale
  • chocolate covered espresso beans
