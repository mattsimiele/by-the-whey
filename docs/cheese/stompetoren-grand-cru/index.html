Gouda

Stompetoren Grand Cru

Made by CONO · North Holland, Netherlands

★ —Not rated yet

Stompetoren cheese is made from meadow milk. Our cows graze and rest in the meadow. As a result, they are 'more comfortable in their own skin' and healthier. Eating fresh grass also ensures a richer, tastier milk. Last year, the cows walked outside for at least 10 hours a day on average 185 days a year. Stompetoren cheese is made in the CONO cheese factory in North Holland. This cheese factory is regarded as the best & greenest cheese factory in the Netherlands. The cheese makers still stir the curd by hand and we use the most modern, sustainable techniques to produce as energy-efficiently as possible.
