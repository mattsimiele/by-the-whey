Afterglow

Made by Blakesville Creamery · Port Washington, Wisconsin, USA

★ 4.04.0 from 1 tasting

Washed in cherry ale and aged to a dense, fudgy texture, Afterglow is a washed-rind goat cheese that brings quiet funk and real character. The beer adds subtle fruit and lift, rounding out the savory depth. It’s structured, aromatic, and always the most talked-about cheese on the board. Blakesville is known for thoughtful, small-batch cheeses.
