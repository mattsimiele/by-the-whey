Sach Paneer

Made by Sach Foods · San Francisco, California, USA

★ —Not rated yet

Rooted in Indian cuisine, paneer is a time-tested, nutrient rich food that’s also a high-protein favorite for millions around the world. It’s a fresh cheese that doesn’t melt. Paneer is rich in healthy fats that keep you full longer. Sach’s Original paneer is keto-friendly, gluten-free, and made with sustainably sourced organic milk that come from small humane certified farms. It is soft, crumbly, and melts in your mouth. With a very neutral taste, our original paneer is created for consumers, including kids, to just snack on. In addition to snacking, we also love using our paneer to cook traditional Indian dishes like Saag Paneer or simply sautéing it with some veggies. Our paneer sears fairly quickly and makes for a quick and delicious weeknight meal. It's also great on the air fryer. **Paneer, like Halloumi, have an ability to stay intact when heated. This melting property (or lack thereof) is attributed to the pH in the milk. For further inquiries regarding the science, please defer to Sarah.
