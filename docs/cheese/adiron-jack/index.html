Adiron-Jack

Made by Nettle Meadow · Warrensburg, NY, USA

Nettle Meadow infuses Adiron-jack with a hint of New York State applejack brandy. Adiron-Jack is a triple-cream made with Jersey cow’s milk. It is not particularly boozy. Nettle Meadow is a sanctuary farm that rehomes retired farm animals in Upstate NY. It is also women owned
