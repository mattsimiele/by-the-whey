Adiron-Jack

Made by Nettle Meadow · Lake Luzerne, NY, USA

★ —Not rated yet

Nettle Meadow infuses Adiron-Jack with a hint of New York State applejack brandy. Adiron-Jack is a triple-creme made with Jersey cow’s milk. It is not particularly boozy. Nettle Meadow is a women owned sanctuary farm that re-homes retired farm animals in Upstate NY.
