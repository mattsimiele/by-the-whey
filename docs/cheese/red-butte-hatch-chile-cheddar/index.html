Red Butte Hatch Chile Cheddar

Made by Bee Hive Cheese · Not provided by creamery, Utah, USA

★ —Not rated yet

Red Butte began as a collaboration with Harmons Neighborhood Grocer during Hatch Chile season. The cheese was such a success that Harmons suggested it should be available to everyone. Today, it is our second-best-selling cheese. Named after Red Butte Garden, a botanical garden at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City, this cheese reflects our deep Utah roots. Beehive Cheese has long supported Red Butte Garden, making the name a natural choice to honor our connection to the community. 1st Place World Cheese Awards 2021 1st Place & Best in Show Utah Cheese Awards 2021 3rd Place International Cheese and Dairy Awards 2021
