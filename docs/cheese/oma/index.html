Oma

Made by Von Trapp Farmstead · Waitsfield, Vermont, USA

★ —Not rated yet

The von Trapp family has run the farmstead since 1959, when Werner and Erika von Trapp moved to Waitsfield, Vermont to grow crops and raise livestock, and to build a sustainable future for their children. The von Trapp Farmstead started making cheese in 2009. Molly Semler grew up in Middlesex, Vermont and returned to the state to follow her passion for making cheese. She started working on the farm over a decade ago, and over the course of making cheese together, she and Sebastian fell in love. (Cheesy, but true!) Sebastian currently runs the farm while Molly operates the creamery.
