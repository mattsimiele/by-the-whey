Delete your account

The fastest way to delete your By the Whey account is inside the app: open Profile → Safety, privacy & support → Safety → Delete my account.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@thecurdnerd.com from the email address connected to your account. Use the subject “By the Whey account deletion.” We will verify account ownership before deletion.

What is deleted

Your profile, authentication account, tastings, comments, likes, follows, blocks, saved cheeses, reports associated with your account, profile image, and uploaded tasting images are permanently removed. Aggregated operational records may be retained only when legally required or necessary to prevent fraud and abuse.

Timing

In-app deletion begins immediately. Verified email requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible and normally within 30 days.

Questions? Contact support@thecurdnerd.com.
