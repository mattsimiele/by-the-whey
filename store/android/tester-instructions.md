# By the Whey closed-beta tester instructions

Thank you for testing By the Whey.

## Join

1. Open the private Google Play opt-in link while signed into the Google account invited to the test.
2. Choose **Become a tester**.
3. Install or update By the Whey from the Play Store link.
4. Remain opted in throughout the beta period.

## First-session test

1. Create an account and complete your profile.
2. Search for a familiar cheese.
3. Save it for later.
4. Log a tasting with a half-star rating, notes, a location, visibility, and optionally a photo.
5. Confirm the tasting appears in Feed and Cellar as expected.
6. Follow another tester, like a tasting, and leave a comment.
7. Edit and then delete a disposable tasting.
8. Enable airplane mode and confirm the saved catalog/feed appears with an offline message.

## Report feedback

Include:

- Phone model
- Android version
- App build/version
- Account role
- What you expected
- What happened
- Reproduction steps
- Screenshot or screen recording when possible

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or sensitive personal information.

Send feedback to support@thecurdnerd.com with subject **By the Whey Android beta**.
