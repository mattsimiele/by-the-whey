# Google Play screenshot plan

Capture screenshots from the production-style Android preview build, not the iOS simulator. Use a clean turophile test account and approved, rights-cleared cheese photography.

Recommended portrait sequence:

1. **Discover exceptional cheese** — Discover list with real catalog photos and category filters
2. **Remember every tasting** — Log screen with a selected cheese, half-star rating, and concise notes
3. **Your personal cheese cellar** — Cellar tasted view with meaningful entries
4. **See what the community loves** — Feed with approved tasting photos and varied users
5. **Every detail, pairing, and story** — Cheese detail page with catalog photo and complete information
6. **Save your next favorite** — Cellar want-to-try view
7. **Built for thoughtful community** — Public profile or comments with no sensitive information
8. **Your data, your control** — Safety & Legal screen showing reporting, blocking, and deletion

## Capture rules

- Use one consistent Android device size, ideally 1080 × 2400 or another common phone resolution.
- Show no simulator controls, debug menus, console errors, email addresses, or test passwords.
- Use realistic but non-sensitive names and locations.
- Ensure uploaded photos are owned by The Curd Nerd or licensed for store marketing.
- Keep the status bar clean and use a consistent time.
- Do not add feature claims that the build does not support.
- Store original screenshots under `store/android/assets/screenshots/`.

The generated feature graphic is `store/android/assets/feature-graphic.png` at the required 1024 × 500 dimensions.
