# Play Console app access instructions

The main catalog can be browsed without an account. Account-dependent features require authentication.

For review, create a dedicated standard turophile account in Supabase and enter its credentials in Play Console → App content → App access. Do not store reviewer passwords in this repository.

Reviewer path:

1. Launch By the Whey.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. Enter the review credentials supplied privately in Play Console.
4. Use Discover to open cheese details.
5. Use Log to create a private or public tasting.
6. Use Cellar to confirm the tasting and saved-cheese state.
7. Use Profile → Safety, privacy & support to view policies, blocking, support, and account deletion.

Administrative and cheesemonger functions are role-restricted and are not required to review the standard consumer experience.
