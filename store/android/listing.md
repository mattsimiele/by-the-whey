## Full description

By the Whey is a modern cheese journal and community built by The Curd Nerd.

Explore a detailed catalog of cheeses from celebrated creameries, record what you taste, save cheeses for later, and remember the flavors, pairings, places, and people behind every wheel.

With By the Whey you can:

- Browse cheeses by style, rating, popularity, recency, and name
- Search by cheese, creamery, and region
- Log half-star ratings, tasting notes, locations, visibility, and photos
- Build a personal Cellar of tasted and saved cheeses
- Follow other cheese lovers and discuss public tastings
- See community ratings based on real tasting activity
- Contribute missing cheeses as an approved cheesemonger
- Control visibility, blocking, reports, and account deletion
- Revisit your saved catalog and feed when your connection drops

Whether you are cheese-curious, a dedicated turophile, or a working cheesemonger, By the Whey helps you find your next favorite and remember why it mattered.

Built by The Curd Nerd.
