# Contributions

By the Whey is proprietary software owned by The Curd Nerd LLC and is not currently accepting unsolicited code, catalog, photography, artwork, or other contributions through public pull requests.

Before submitting any proposed contribution, contact support@thecurdnerd.com. A contribution may be accepted only after the contributor and The Curd Nerd LLC agree in writing to appropriate ownership or licensing terms. Do not submit confidential information or material owned by another person or organization.

Bug reports and beta feedback that do not include proprietary third-party material are welcome through the project's designated support channels.
